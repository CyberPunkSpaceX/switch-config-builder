1. Start from a template

New to EVPN-VXLAN? Pick the template closest to your network. It fills in every step below, and you can change anything afterwards. The wizard works out loopbacks, link addresses, AS numbers, VNIs, route targets, gateways and ESIs for you and writes one config per switch.

What is EVPN-VXLAN, in plain words?

The switches are connected by normal routed links (the underlay). Each switch has a loopback address, and the underlay makes sure every switch can reach every other loopback.

VXLAN carries your VLANs inside those routed links, so a VLAN can exist on every switch without spanning tree. Every VLAN gets a number called a VNI. The switches that wrap and unwrap the traffic are VTEPs.

EVPN is BGP telling every VTEP which MAC and IP addresses live where (the overlay). A VRF keeps groups of VLANs (for example staff and guests) apart when they are routed. The anycast gateway is the same default gateway address answered by several switches at once.

        EVPN-VXLAN
        
      
        Stretches VLANs over a routed (layer 3) network. Each switch needs a loopback and AS (Routing tab) and an underlay that reaches the other loopbacks (OSPF or BGP on the OSPF & BGP tab, MTU 9216 on fabric links). Then give VLANs a VNI on the VLANs tab. Runs on the EX4100, EX4400 and EX4300-48MP, with a feature license.
        
      
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
      
        
      
        On the VLANs tab: VNI stretches the VLAN, anycast gateway is the shared default gateway all leaves answer for (each leaf keeps its own IRB address in the same subnet), and VRF puts the VLAN's IRB in a tenant VRF. On the LAGs tab, an ESI and LACP system ID make a LAG span two switches (ESI-LAG multihoming).

        QoS for voice and video
        
      
        When a link is busy, QoS lets calls and video go first so they don't break up. Phones mark voice as EF (DSCP 46) and video as AF41 (DSCP 34). The switch puts voice in a strict-priority queue, gives video its own share, keeps some bandwidth for network control, and gives the rest to everything else. Pair it with a voice VLAN on your phone ports and LLDP-MED.
